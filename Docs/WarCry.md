# War Cry

During a human attack gesture, shout while the HUD says DRAG. The microphone is opened during the countdown to allow startup, but only the drawing phase contributes to the bonus. Defense gestures do not use the microphone. Review freezes the bonus, Redo discards it, and Confirm stores it for that player's attack. While drawing, the headline shows the damage multiplier in gold (always "x1.25", whatever the phone's language) and the seconds left. The weapon gains procedural lightning in the player's colour that grows with the bonus and remains for the whole round. While the microphone records, the music drops to 10% so the cry is not the music played back.

The microphone's RMS amplitude is converted with `20 * log10(RMS)` into dBFS. These are digital levels, not calibrated physical dB SPL. The loudest sampled window during the gesture determines power. Defaults: -35 dBFS or below gives x1 damage, -21.5 gives x1.25, and -8 or above gives x1.5 (`WarCryRules.MaxBonus`). The WarCryMicrophone component exposes both thresholds in the Inspector during Play; attach/configure it on the arena manager to persist tuning. Ordinary loud ambient sounds also count: this detects volume, not speech or the identity of the person shouting.

The cry itself is played back so its loudest moment lands on that attack's hit in the clash. The microphone records from Detect pen (countdown included) until 1.5 s after the stroke is recognised, so a shout that starts early or goes on is not cut. `WarCryMicrophone.TakeCry` then keeps the shout from `CryLead` (0.25 s) before its loudest moment to its end (through breaths of up to 0.3 s, at most 3.5 s), evens out its level and keeps it as an in-memory clip; Confirm stores it for the player's attack (ending the tail early if needed), Redo or Cancel discards it. Only the drag counts for the damage bonus. `ClashDirector` estimates when the blade will connect from the swing's pace and starts the clip `CryLead` earlier. It plays louder with a stronger cry, and is freed at the end of the round.

Damage applies weapon class advantage, blocking and the cry multiplier before rounding once. Each attack has its own bonus, cleared between rounds and rematches. Computer attacks generate random digital levels from -45 to -5 dBFS, passed through the same power curve, in both P v C and C v C. Computers do not open the microphone and have no cry to play back.

Android requests microphone permission at first human attack capture; iOS uses the microphone usage description in Player Settings. Permission denial, no input device, or startup failure leaves normal damage available. Audio stays in memory only (the recording of the current gesture and the confirmed cries of the current round) and is never saved to disk or uploaded. Recording stops 1.5 s after the stroke (or on Confirm), on Redo, cancellation, failed capture, disabling the component, application pause, or loss of focus.

Validation: compile Unity scripts for Editor and Android conditional branches, then run Tests/Run-DuelMatchTests.ps1 (DuelMatch.cs, PenStroke.cs and WarCryRules.cs). On a physical phone, allow the microphone, record quiet/loud attacks, confirm or redo, hear the cry peak on the hit, compare actual damage, verify lightning follows the weapon in the clash, and test denied permissions plus both computer modes. Device audio and shader rendering require live Unity/device validation.
