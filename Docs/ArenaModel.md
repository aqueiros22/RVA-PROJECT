# Arena model

The imported FBX root keeps its original rotation and scale, including Unity's axis conversion. Arena Orientation Offset defaults to zero and applies an optional extra rotation on a separate parent before bounds/floor measurement. Changing it during Play recreates and measures the arena immediately. Arena Yaw controls its heading between the players.

`CardWeaponManager` shows one arena when both player avatars are visible, after menu/player setup. It uses their smoothed positions and table plane, centres the model's bounds between them, and scales its shortest horizontal dimension to cover their separation plus a margin. It hides when either avatar disappears. During a duel it follows the same held poses as the avatars, including temporary card occlusion.

`Assets/Editor/ArenaModelBinding.cs` assigns `Assets/Arena Model/3d-model.fbx` to an empty Arena Prefab field when scenes open, before Play, and when building. A manually assigned prefab takes precedence. Save the scene to persist the assignment.

Select the object with CardWeaponManager to tune Arena Size Per Player Distance (default 1.8), Arena Minimum Size In Cards (4), and Arena Yaw. Auto Detect Floor finds the highest near-horizontal mesh surface at the arena centre. Arena Depth Below Cards places that floor below the players by 0.15 card lengths by default, leaving the physical cards above the arena floor; increase it to lower the arena further. Players remain at their original card positions. The imported arena has Read/Write enabled for this one-time measurement. Disable Auto Detect Floor to use Arena Floor Fraction instead (floor height as a fraction of the total model bounds). Imported materials must support URP.

Device validation: finish setup; show only player1 (no arena), then both cards (one arena centred between them). Move them apart/together and rotate their shared plane; verify centring, scale, floor height and materials. Cover a card during picking (arena hides after tracking grace), then during a duel (arena stays with the held avatars). Repeat with player2 detected first and in the Android build.

The original FBX references four unavailable JPGs from its author's computer. Replacement materials in `Assets/Arena Model` are explicitly remapped in the FBX importer: Dibujo3 → Arena Sand, Stone_Flagstone_Ashlar → Arena Stone, Plaza de toros. caceres → Arena Plaster, Color_A11 → Arena Trim, and Roofing_Tile_Spanish → Arena Terracotta. `Arena/Procedural Surface` renders grain, masonry joints and roof tiles directly in URP without external image textures. Each material exposes Surface Colour, Detail Colour, Pattern and Pattern Repeat. These are substitute finishes, not the original author's textures. Check the imported model in Unity for correct slot placement, pattern scale and shader rendering on Android.
